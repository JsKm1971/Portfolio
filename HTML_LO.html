Leadership styles

Visionary

Long-term direction

Core principle

"Come with me." Mobilises people toward a shared vision, giving freedom on how to get there.

Coaching

People development

Core principle

"Try this." Connects daily work to long-term goals, fostering growth and capability over time.

Democratic

Collaborative input

Core principle

"What do you think?" Builds consensus and commitment by involving the team in decisions.

Pacesetting

High performance

Core principle

"Do as I do." Sets a high bar by example — most effective with motivated, skilled teams.
